## The savings stack that actually works at Best Buy

Best Buy discounts its own inventory constantly; you just need to know where the pricing lives. These channels require no code at all:

- **Top Deals** — weekly promotions across TVs, appliances, accessories, and more.

Lower grades may show cosmetic wear, damaged packaging, or missing non-essential accessories, but for laptops, TVs, and appliances this is often the steepest legitimate discount in the store. Whether a paid tier makes sense depends on how much you buy; for a single TV or laptop purchase, the free tier plus sale pricing usually covers it.

## Price match: the most underrated Best Buy "coupon"

Best Buy's Price Match Guarantee functions like a coupon you don't have to find. If an identical item (same brand, model, and color, in stock) is cheaper at a qualifying competitor, Best Buy matches the price. Qualifying retailers include Amazon, Apple, B&H Photo Video, Costco, Crutchfield, Target, and Walmart.

The exclusions are where coupon users get burned, though. Price matching does **not** apply to coupon offers, clearance items, open-box or refurbished products, Marketplace items, or certain limited-time promotions. Practically: if a competitor's low price comes from *their* coupon code, Best Buy won't match it — but if it's a straight sale price, they will. Between the two, a plain sale price at a competitor is more valuable to you than a code.
